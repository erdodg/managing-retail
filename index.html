Opening · The concept

What is retailing?

Before the case, the definition every later slide builds on.

Indonesia's battleground ≈37%

of modern grocery trade flows through minimarkets*

“Retailing includes all the activities involved in selling goods or services directly to final consumers for personal, non-business use.”

Kotler, Keller & Chernev (2022), Ch.16

1

A retailer's core role

Bridging producers and consumers: breaking bulk, offering assortment and convenience.

2

Where the shopper decides

The store, physical or digital, is where positioning, price and experience meet demand.

3

Why it matters here

Minimarkets are the decisive channel, and two chains dominate most of it.

KEY IDEARetailing is where strategy becomes a sale. In Indonesia, that moment usually happens inside a minimarket.

Definition: Kotler, Keller & Chernev (2022), Marketing Management 16e, Ch.16. *≈37% is the group's research figure; verify the source before presenting.

Act 02 · Entrance

Service level & store atmosphere

How much help the store gives, and the physical mood that makes customers comfortable enough to buy.

Self-serviceLimited serviceFull service

Alfamart sits here: self-selection, staff help when needed, plus bill payment, top-ups and parcel services.

Store atmosphere checklist
  • Clear layout and signs: can I find it in seconds?
  • Clean and bright: do I feel comfortable?
  • Tidy checkout: is the queue still under control?
  • Local themes are fine if the store stays easy to read.

People are part of atmosphere. A flagship can look premium and still stay easy to read: that is the test.

KEY IDEAService and atmosphere are part of the offer: fast, clean and readable beats fancy for daily missions.

Theory: levels of service & store atmosphere (KKC Ch.16; services Ch.9). Photos: social media / alfamart.co.id.

Act 04 · Checkout

Communication & omnichannel

One customer identity joins every channel. Multichannel only offers many channels with separate benefits.

Multichannel
  • Store, app, website: all present
  • Benefits and info are separate
  • Customer recognised differently per channel
Omnichannel: Alfamart
  • One member: store + Alfagift + A-Poin
  • Identity and benefits integrated
  • Stock and promos consistent across channels
±8%

of AMRT sales via Alfagift (management, Jun 2026). KISI estimates <5%: "online" is defined differently.

One offer, read once: product & size · price & period · terms & channel
Catalog›Shelf›Checkout›App›Delivery / pick-up
KEY IDEAAn offer must be understood in a single read, and say the same thing in every channel.

Sources: Fortune IDN (5 Jun 2026); Kontan (Jun 2026); KISI. Theory: retail communication, omnichannel (KKC Ch.16; IMC Ch.12–14; channels Ch.15).

Theory · KKC Ch.16

The wheel of retailing

New retailers enter low-margin, low-price and low-status, then add services, raise costs, and become open to the next low-price entrant.

Wheel of retailing

2 · Add value

3 · Trade up

1 · Enter low

1

Enter low: O!Save, KopDes

Low price, low margin, no-frills basics

2

Add value: Alfamart & Indomaret today

Fresh food, coffee, Lawson ready-to-eat, services, Alfagift

3

Trade up: hypermarkets & premium supermarkets

Big footprint, higher costs: the "weakening middle" now under pressure

KEY IDEAThe real risk: trading up without losing the everyday-value trust that new discounters are targeting.
